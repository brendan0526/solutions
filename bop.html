What's inside a BOP

A BOP combines two policies you'd need to buy separately anyway:

🛡️ General Liability

Covers third-party injury and property damage claims at your location or from your operations

🏢 Commercial Property

Covers your building, equipment, and inventory against fire, theft, and weather damage

Plus: Business Interruption Coverage

If a covered loss forces you to close temporarily, BOP replaces lost income and covers ongoing expenses while you rebuild.

What a BOP covers

In plain language, here's what you're protected against:

🤕

Customer Injury at Your Location

A customer slips and falls — GL inside the BOP covers medical costs and your legal defense.

🔥

Fire, Theft & Weather Damage

Your building, equipment, and inventory are covered against common perils under the property component.

🚫

Forced Business Closure

If a fire or covered event shuts you down, business interruption coverage replaces lost revenue while you're closed.

⚡

Equipment Breakdown

Mechanical or electrical breakdown of essential equipment — often added to BOPs for restaurants and retailers.

Common questions

How much does a BOP cost in Arizona?

Most small businesses pay between $500-$2,500 per year for a BOP depending on business type, location, revenue, and property value. Buying GL and property bundled in a BOP is almost always cheaper than purchasing them separately.

Does a BOP cover my employees?

No — a BOP covers third-party claims and your property, not employee injuries. For employee injuries you'll need workers' compensation, which is a separate policy.

Can I add coverage to a BOP?

Yes — BOPs are designed to be customized. Common add-ons include professional liability, cyber liability, liquor liability (for restaurants/bars), and equipment breakdown coverage.

Is a BOP right for a contractor?

Sometimes — it depends on the trade and whether you have a fixed location. Many contractors are better served by a standalone GL policy with separate tools and equipment coverage. We'll tell you which makes more sense for your situation.
