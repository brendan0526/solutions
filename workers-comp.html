What Workers' Comp covers

When an employee is injured on the job, workers' comp covers:

🏥

Medical Expenses

Doctor visits, hospital stays, surgery, and ongoing treatment for work-related injuries or illnesses — covered regardless of fault.

💰

Lost Wages

If an injured employee can't work, workers' comp replaces a portion of their lost income while they recover.

♿

Disability Benefits

Temporary or permanent disability payments if an employee is partially or fully unable to return to work.

⚖️

Employer's Liability

Legal defense costs if an injured employee sues your business beyond the standard workers' comp claim.

Common questions

Is workers' comp required in Arizona?

Yes — Arizona requires workers' compensation coverage for all employers with one or more employees, including part-time and seasonal workers. Sole proprietors without employees are exempt but can elect coverage voluntarily.

How is the premium calculated?

Workers' comp is priced based on your payroll, the type of work your employees do (job classification codes), and your claims history (experience modification factor). We make sure your employees are classified correctly so you're not overpaying.

What happens at audit time?

Most workers' comp policies are audited annually to true up your premium based on actual payroll. We help you prepare for audits and avoid surprises by setting your initial estimate as accurately as possible.

Can I cover subcontractors under my policy?

Generally no — subcontractors should carry their own workers' comp. If they don't, you may be held liable for their injuries. We can help verify subcontractor coverage and advise on your exposure.
