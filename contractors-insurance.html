Common questions

What limits do I need for a GC's certificate?

Most GCs require a minimum of $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate for GL, plus additional insured status. Larger commercial projects often require $2M/$4M or an umbrella. We review your actual contract requirements before quoting so the limits are right from day one.

How fast can I get a certificate of insurance?

Same day in most cases once your policy is bound. If you need to be on a job site tomorrow, call us directly at (480) 436-3529 and we'll make it happen.

Do subcontractors need their own insurance?

Yes — every sub you hire should carry their own GL and workers' comp. If they don't, you may be held liable for their injuries and any damage they cause. We can help you build a subcontractor insurance requirement into your contracts.

I'm a sole proprietor with no employees — what do I need?

At minimum, GL. If you own a truck or use tools that leave your home, you may also need commercial auto and a tools floater. Workers' comp is optional for sole proprietors in Arizona but worth considering if you work on job sites regularly.

Can you cover roofing and other high-risk trades?

Yes — we work with carriers that still actively write roofing, excavation, and other trades that standard markets won't touch. Height work and storm-season capacity are both addressable depending on your specific operation.
