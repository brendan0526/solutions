What General Liability covers

GL protects your business from the most common third-party claims:

🤕

Bodily Injury

A customer slips and falls at your location or job site — GL covers their medical costs and your legal defense.

🏠

Property Damage

You or your crew accidentally damages a client's property — GL covers the repair or replacement cost.

📋

Completed Operations

A claim arises from work you already finished — GL covers you after the job is done, not just while you're on site.

📣

Personal & Advertising Injury

Claims of libel, slander, or copyright infringement in your advertising — covered under a standard GL policy.

Common questions

How much does GL insurance cost in Arizona?

Most small businesses pay between $400-$1,500 per year for a $1M/$2M GL policy. Contractors typically pay more depending on the trade, revenue, and claims history. We shop multiple carriers to find the best rate for your specific situation.

How quickly can I get a certificate of insurance?

Once your policy is bound, we can issue a certificate of insurance the same day — usually within a few hours. If you need to be on a job site tomorrow, call us directly at (480) 436-3529.

What limits do I need?

Most GCs and landlords require a minimum of $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate. Some larger contracts require $2M/$4M. We review your contract requirements before quoting so the limits are right from day one.

Do I need GL if I work from home?

Yes — if you meet clients, visit job sites, or provide any service to paying customers, you have GL exposure regardless of where your office is.
