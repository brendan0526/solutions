What Commercial Auto covers

Commercial auto covers vehicles used for business in ways personal policies specifically exclude:

🚗

Auto Liability

Covers bodily injury and property damage you cause to others while driving a business vehicle — required by Arizona law.

🔧

Physical Damage

Comprehensive and collision coverage for your business vehicles — repairs or replaces them after an accident, theft, or weather damage.

🚐

Common questions

Does my personal auto policy cover my work truck?

No — personal auto policies specifically exclude vehicles used primarily for business. If you're using a truck or van for work and only have personal coverage, you likely have no coverage for business-related accidents.

What if my employees drive their own cars for work?

Their personal policies may not cover business use. Hired & Non-Owned Auto coverage protects your business from liability when employees use personal or rented vehicles for work purposes.

Can I insure multiple vehicles under one policy?

Yes — fleet policies cover multiple vehicles under one program, often at a better rate than insuring each separately. We can structure a fleet policy for 2 vehicles or 20+.

Do you cover specialty vehicles and trailers?

Yes — we can cover pickups, box trucks, cargo vans, flatbeds, trailers, and most commercial vehicles. Let us know what you're running and we'll find the right market for it.
